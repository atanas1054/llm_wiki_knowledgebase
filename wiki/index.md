Also the clearest measurement of a **capacity conflict at a discrete bottleneck**: adding geometry to a fixed 16384 codebook costs 2.61 PSNR and doubles the semantic discrepancy; multi-codebook repairs most but not all of it. **The "unified interface" is forked in practice** — planning uses the geometry tokenizer, world modelling uses the representation-only one, and no run shows a single tokenizer doing both. Generation results appear in a figure with no numbers |
| [ReWorld](sources/reworld.md) | HUST + Xiaomi EV; **the direct sequel to [DriveLaW](sources/drivelaw.md) from the same group**, and the first paper here to ask what should be *true of* a WAM's intermediate latents rather than where the planner reads them. Three teacher-free objectives at **1.003× training cost**: an auxiliary future-flow head at Video DiT block 8, cosine alignment of Action DiT states to their stop-gradient attended readout, and repulsion from the nearest simulator-scored unsafe trajectory. **FVD 81.3 → 61.9 and FID 4.6 → 4.4 (first entry to lead both), PDMS 89.1 → 90.4 with no RL, UCF-101 probe 68.3 → 80.2.** But **17.0 of the 19.4 FVD gain is an inference-time guidance scale**, and its planning ablation attributes the whole +1.3 PDMS to two *action-side* objectives with **no row measuring the video-side one** — making it the wiki's first intra-paper evidence that generation quality and planning quality are decoupled. | [LWDrive](sources/lwdrive.md) | Chongqing University; the VLM output is an *intent anchor*, not a trajectory — a six-stage Foresight Cascade Planner refines a proposal pool, each stage reading a different Qwen2.5-VL layer through Bridge Attention plus BEV residual updates, then a simulator-distilled score head selects. **92.0 PDMS v1 / 89.6 EPDMS v2 with no RL of any kind.** Two results, both awkward for its own framing: **layer-wise readout is worth +0.2** (against Adaptive-WAM's +4.80 on a video DiT and GeoWorldAD's +1.1 on a geometry decoder) and is a safety-for-progress trade, not a gain; and the **world-model column is never varied with the refinement stack present**, so the +1.8 it claims for future-frame supervision is measured on a bare VLM decoder. **EP 90.3 on v2 is the highest in the wiki and beats the human agent's 87.4** — at EC 73.3. No latency, no parameter count, no $N_p$ |
| [WCog-VLA](sources/wcog-vla.md) | Tongji + NTU; the only world model here that forecasts **other agents' trajectories** rather than the scene — a joint multi-agent diffusion (ADDT) conditioned on an InternVL3-2B VLM with BEV/TrackFormer agent tokens, plus 85k Stackelberg **Game-CoT** annotations and DiffGRPO. **92.9 PDMS at 2B** (4th in the wiki, best per-parameter); NC 99.4 / TTC 98.5 both 2nd-highest. | [DriveFuture](sources/drivefuture.md) | CASIA + five others; the future latent as a planning **condition** rather than a prediction target — **no future-prediction loss anywhere**, only trajectory-denoising gradients plus a training-time cross-attention grounding against the real future BEV, annealed to zero by a sigmoid schedule. **55.5 EPDMS navhard (1st on the leaderboard), 89.9 corrected EPDMS v2, 90.7 PDMS v1.** But **the 55.5 decomposes as 34.6 model + 20.9 GTRS-Dense scorer**, recoverable because its ablation row's submetrics match its unscored stage-wise row exactly — the wiki's first in-paper price for a scorer. Implicit conditioning beats MSE regression by +2.5, but regression is **+1.2 over the null here** where WA-JEPA measured it negative. **Three of nine swept settings fall below the no-future baseline**, and holding the training oracle to 95% of training (28.9) is worse than never using it — the training-side twin of DriveLaW's t=10 collapse. Its Table 1 is the wiki's largest navhard leaderboard and shares no method with GeoWAM's. No latency, no parameter count, no code |
| [CoWorld-VLA](sources/coworld-vla.md) | Afari Intelligent Drive + Southeast Univ. + others — **the same lab as [WA-JEPA](sources/wa-jepa.md), ten of eleven authors shared, same GitHub org — yet CoWorld-VLA never cites it.** Four future-prediction objectives run in parallel as typed VLM tokens — V-JEPA semantics, VGGT geometry, a Wan2.2-5B DiT used as a **differentiable critic on one token**, and GT trajectory — fused by a four-branch diffusion planner. **90.0 PDMS v1 / 90.0 corrected EPDMS v2 / FVD 32.7**, from a **single front-camera frame** on Qwen3-VL-2B with no RL and no scorer. Contributes the wiki's **first complete representation × planner 2×2**: each factor is worth ~+5 alone and ~+1 on top of the other (**interaction −3.9**), so representation quality and planner capacity are largely *substitutes*. Also the second seed-variance measurement (std 0.013) and the **first paper to name the training-seed gap**. Its evaluator-column labels **contradict its own lab's WA-JEPA** on DriveVLA-W0, and it publishes three v2 numbers for methods that never reported any. No latency, two frozen encoders in the inference path |
| [Drive-HWM](sources/drive-hwm.md) | Beihang + others; **two world models on two clocks, with two different prediction targets** — a V-JEPA slow branch predicting eight **optical-flow** fields in parallel (25.6 ms, amortized to 3.2) and an Emu3-8B fast branch predicting the next **RGB** frame plus the action, coupled by FiLM. **The paper reports two mutually inconsistent result sets: 93.8 PDMS / 86.4 EPDMS in every table, 93.3 / 86.2 in every sentence** — which decides whether it leads the wiki's v1 ladder or ties fourth, and whether its own hierarchy is worth +0.8 or +0.3. Its real contribution is the **target-role split**: the long-horizon branch wants motion and the short-horizon branch wants appearance, ablated independently in one codebase — a *target-content* rule orthogonal to the wiki's *objective-form* rule. Also supplies the **video-pretrained non-JEPA control** [[concepts/foundation-backbones-for-ad.md]] has been asking for (V-JEPA 93.8 > WAN 93.2 > CogVideo 93.0, all three video-pretrained), and a clean five-way conditioning sweep where **FiLM beats concatenation by 1.3 — more than the hierarchy itself**. But **NAVSIM is single-shot, so with $N{=}8$ the slow model fires once per scenario and the high-frequency re-decision the design exists for never happens**; "one action" is never defined, and the two readings differ by 8× in latency. Seventh user of the shared v2 baseline block, which it renames "DriveSupervisor" — the first direct evidence the block is copied, not recomputed. Two of four ablation rows equal DriveVLA-W0's published rows. No code, no parameter counts, no generation metrics, 256×144 input |
| [Qwen-Drive-1.0](sources/qwen-drive-1.0.md) | Qwen Team + HUST; the wiki's first **released foundation model** (4B weights + code) rather than a planner. Qwen3.5-4B kept **architecturally unchanged**, with an external BEV perception head and a **cached-KV flow-matching Planning Expert**; four stages, the last an RL stage that turns a deterministic Euler flow into a policy by perturbing only the last 3 of 10 steps inside a **6-mode cosine subspace**. **90.7 PDMS / 91.4 BoN-6, 7.91 RFS WOD-E2E test, 43.95 mAP nuScenes.** Three results matter more than the scores. **(1) General-capability retention, measured across 15 benchmarks and 13 models**: 66.41 vs. its base model's 67.40, while Alpamayo-1.5 scores 14.65, MiMo-Embodied 26.53 and UniDriveVLA 48.73 — and the *unadapted* Qwen3.5-4B beats every driving-specialized model on driving QA. **(2) A 3D probe**: a converged BEV head on frozen VL features trails a dedicated detector on the *same* features by 6.34 mAP; unfreezing recovers +10.46 — vision-language pretraining does not contain driving 3D structure. **(3) The wiki's first closed-loop reproduction of NAVSIM leaders**: in AlpaSim, SimWAM (91.5 PDMS) is *last* on at-fault score and Alpamayo-R1, which reports no NAVSIM number, is first. Its own Table 8 prices the entire driving adaptation at **+0.08 RFS** for planning. Occupancy is its one perception loss; no latency anywhere; 55.9% of the public driving-VQA corpus failed its consistency filter |
| [GRAVA](sources/grava.md) | Purely autoregressive Qwen3-VL-8B VLA with one front camera. It writes **grounded reasoning** (box tokens plus ego-frame distance, speed and heading for each action-relevant object, then per-object interaction and decision, then an ego decision) and follows it in the same stream with a **4-primitive Executable Planner action** (STOP / CRAWL / CURVE / CRUISE plus parameters) that a fixed geometric decoder turns into waypoints. Targets come from an offline agentic, trajectory-anchored DAG; the resulting GR-NavSim has 2.2M QA pairs and 70K traces. Training: GRA pre-training → planner warm-up → verified self-distillation → **Active RL**, i.e. DAPO-style GRPO on scenes re-screened each loop as "recoverable" (4,676 of 102,861; 21.8% overlap between loops). **90.48 PDMS**, but **pre-RL 82.10**, so RL is worth +8.38 and mostly repairs safety (DAC +6.4, TTC +6.1). "Best purely AR" rests on +0.18 in its own table and omits DynVLA 91.7. **The wiki's most graded CoT ablation** is on an internal benchmark only: action-only 74.8 → coarse 77.9 → grounded objects 85.5 → full 90.1 CDS; without RL, full GRA scores 73.1. Editing the trace edits the action (0.44 → 0.82), **but the trace's ego decision is copied from the expert trajectory's intent**. Executable Planner vs. direct waypoints: +3.25. No latency, no v2, no code; five figures missing |
| [SUV](sources/suv.md) | XJTU + USTC + Yinwang + Fudan; code announced. **Future scene understanding as video generation.** One Wan2.2-5B video expert generates **four future streams** as native video: RGB, SAM 3 segmentation, DA3 relative depth and SAM 3 instance tracks, the last three rendered as colored videos through the same frozen VAE, with no stream-specific heads. A ~1B MoT action expert reads all stream latents under a directed mask (streams cannot see each other; the future cannot see the action). **91.0 EPDMS v2 (corrected; second in the wiki behind the omitted WA-JEPA 91.7) / 36.9 navhard (second unscored, behind SpanVLA 40.1) / 7.94 RFS WOD-E2E / 90.8 PDMS v1**, with no RL and no scorer; 2 solver steps give 91.0 at 288 ms. **Key result: the 2×2 supervision × access ablation.** Structured supervision helps without access (+1.0 / +2.3), and **future access is worth +0.3 on navtest but +4.1 on navhard**. That reproduces SimWAM's navtest null and suggests navtest cannot detect the effect. Caveats: uncited same-backbone predecessors (SimWAM 91.5, DriveVA 90.9 v1, both above it); a v2 table captioned "corrected" that carries a pre-fix row; future metrics measured as agreement with the teachers only; single runs |
| [Metis](sources/metis.md) | Fudan + SII + Li Auto + HKU + Imperial + Surrey; code released. A Wan2.2-5B video expert plus a ~1B action expert in a MoT with an **asymmetric mask: the future video reads the action; the action never reads the future**. Video is dropped at inference (147 ms at 2 steps). **89.5 EPDMS v2 / 32.2 navhard / 89.1 PDMS v1** (best-of-6: 90.3 / 89.7), plus CityWalker urban navigation and a zero-shot Unitree Go2 demo. Mask ablation: +0.5 navtest but **+2.2 navhard** over isolated; bidirectional is worst. It completes a **three-paper mask family** with SimWAM and SUV on the same backbone and recipe, where every mechanism is invisible on navtest and large on navhard. Caveats: **its v2 table uses pre-fix anchors while its own row's residual looks corrected**, so its "+2.4 over VLA" becomes −0.2 against DriveFine's corrected 89.7; v1 "SOTA" relies on best-of-6; CityWalker is second to ABot-N0 overall; Table 10's navhard columns duplicate Table 5's; Table 6 mislabels the 5B model as "Wan2.2-14B"; the 8× speedup conflates step count with video generation (2.9× at matched steps) |
| [Hydra-MDP++](sources/hydra-mdp-pp.md) | NVIDIA + ECNU + Fudan; code released. **Ancestor of the selection-based cohort.** A ResNet-34 / V2-99 image encoder scores a fixed 8192-trajectory vocabulary with BCE heads distilled from **offline PDM-simulator sub-scores (ground-truth perception)**, then picks the lowest weighted log-cost (grid-searched weights, +1.5 PDMS, its largest ablation). **86.6 / 91.0 PDMS v1** (206 / 271 ms on V100). Introduced TL/DDC/LK/EC, but **its "EPDMS" is a different formula from NAVSIM-v2's** (C not HC, LK weight 5 with a strict 0.5 m definition, /22). Its rows (TransFuser 77.8, VADv2 76.6, Hydra-MDP 79.8, Hydra-MDP++ 80.6) recur in ReCogDrive, SGDrive and WCog-VLA v2 tables, with C relabelled HC. This resolves WCog-VLA's "fourth set" puzzle and adds a third convention lineage. The 91.0 PDMS and 84.1 EPDMS headlines come from two different models. Auxiliary perception hurts (−0.5) |
